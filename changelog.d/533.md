- **The browser gate's grants read alike in every place (#533).** For #533: the CLI's `origins` (text and `--json`), the owner's `GET /origins`, the MCP's `list_origins` and the inspector's origins view and `GET /api/origins` read a grant's expiry, state, ceiling and month's spend from one reading of the row (`GrantShown`), so an expired row reads `expired` and a spending row whose month has reached its ceiling reads so in each. Each grant row's JSON gains `state` (`granted`, `expired` or `revoked`) and `ceilingReached` (a spending row's tally at or past its ceiling; `null` for another kind); every text line shows its grant time and expiry, a spending row with no ceiling reads `no ceiling` (it read `0/month`), and a full month reads `at the ceiling`.

- **`gift-claim` of a gift whose deposit is not mined yet records its coin by itself once the claim is mined (#638).** Such a claim is posted at once, and the wallet's door declines its record while the deposit has no checked proof (`Invalid BEEF at byte 48: InputNamesNoElement`); through 0.19.0-beta.10 nothing kept that record, so no later pass made it and the coin stayed out of the balance until `receive` was run by hand. Now the claim's tracker row keeps the record from the moment a broadcaster takes the claim until the wallet has made it, and the tracker pass makes it once the claim's own proof checks: both outputs (the gift and the change of the fee coin) through `internalizeAction`, counted once. `daemon` and `serve` run that pass (every minute by default; `BROADCAST_RECONCILE=0` turns it off); with neither running, `bsv-wallet tracker-tick` is one such pass. `receive <claim txid>` and `receive <claim txid> --vout 1` by hand still work once the claim is mined, before a pass or after one; a pass after them takes in what is left and changes nothing else, and an output the wallet has spent stays spent. A restart between the post and the proof loses nothing: the record is in the wallet's own file.
- **`gift-claim` says what happened, in text and in JSON (#638).** A claim that is posted and not recorded yet printed one of the two commands that take its coin in, and under `--json` no hint at all. The text now says that the claim is posted, that its coin is not in the balance and why (no checked proof of the gift's deposit was to be had, with the door's own words), that the wallet keeps the record and who makes it, and both commands. Under `--json` three fields carry the same: `recordHeld` (`true` when the coin is not recorded yet and the wallet keeps the claim's record), `recordHint` (the sentence, `null` when recorded) and `receiveCommands` (the commands that take the coin in by hand, one per output of the claim that pays the wallet; empty when recorded). `recorded` and `recordError` are unchanged. The exit code is unchanged, 0 whenever the claim is posted or held: the claim is on the network, and a failure's exit would invite a second claim. GIFT.md lists every field.
- **The tracker pass names the records it keeps (#638, #620).** `tracker-tick` prints an `owed:` line (`owed` under `--json`) for each record the wallet is owed and has not made: a claim not mined yet, with its word; a claim the network refused (`rejected`, `conflicted`, `abandoned`), which leaves no coin and says so; a record tried sixteen times, with the command that records it by hand. A try that does not make the record waits a pause that doubles. With no chain tracker (`CHAINTRACKS_URL=off`) a mined claim's record is not handed over and the pass names it in its faults. A claim held as not final (`gift-claim --force` before the lock time) whose re-announce is accepted no longer loses its record when the wallet declines it at that moment: the record stays on the row and is made once the claim is mined; the pass's line for it now ends `its record is kept, and a pass makes it once it is mined, or bsv-wallet receive <txid> records it by hand then`. A record the wallet makes is logged at info with the marker `own_transaction_recorded`.
- **For a wallet that claimed on an earlier version (#638).** Its row holds no record, so nothing makes it later: once the claim is mined, run `bsv-wallet receive <claim txid>` and `bsv-wallet receive <claim txid> --vout 1`.
- **The library's surface (#638).** `tracker_host::heard` takes what the row keeps as a `Keep` (`Nothing`, `Reannounce`, `Record`) where it took an `Option<Reannounce>`; `tracker_host::record_accepted` takes the storage beside the wallet; `tracker_host::record_made`, `RECORD_HELD_WITHOUT_A_TRACKER` and `TickReport::owed` are new, as are `store::owed_records`, `set_owed_tries` and `drop_owed_record`. The tally of a record's tries is a `tracker_meta` row keyed `owed:<txid>`; no table or column is added.

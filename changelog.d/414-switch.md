- **The switch: every served door admits a page by the owner's grant per power from the store (#414, the design's L5b, under the owner's rulings W1 (a), W4 (a) and W15 (i) of 2026-11-03):** a door asks the powers it needs (a basket, a protocol at its level with its counterparty, a label, a certificate's fields, a spend) and admits a page whose origin holds a live grant row of each. A power with no row, or an expired one, holds the call on one pending request until the owner answers it from `bsv-wallet origin-grant` or `origin-deny`, the owner's routes, `bsv-wallet-mcp`'s `answer_origin` or the inspector; a deny answers 400 `ERR_PERMISSION_DENIED` `Permission denied.`, a revoked power is refused at once. A page that closes its connection while held is denied with its origin's other open requests, which are deleted and kept nowhere; a restart withdraws every open request, and a request's number is never given again. At most 2 calls per origin and 4 in all are held; past either a call is answered 429 `ERR_PERMISSION_PENDING_LIMIT` with `Retry-After: 5`. At shutdown a held call is answered 503 `WALLET_BRIDGE_UNAVAILABLE`. `isAuthenticated`, `waitForAuthentication`, `getHeight`, `getHeaderForHeight`, `getNetwork`, `getVersion` and a protocol at security level 0 answer any page with an `Origin` with no row; the admin-reserved names are refused at once. Every answer to a parsed `Origin` carries its CORS echo and its preflight is answered. A spend is answered once, granted with a monthly ceiling or denied (the spending power, `414-spending.md`).
- **`ALLOWED_ORIGINS` is now a seed, and how each caller it lists moves:** a listed originator is admitted at every door and host route as before, with no grant row, and is logged by name at the start of `serve` and `daemon` (`origin grants: ALLOWED_ORIGINS lists app.example:8443; during the seed window it is admitted at every door as (b) admits it, with no grant row; its powers are asked of the owner once the seed is retired (#414 L7)`) with the notice `ALLOWED_ORIGINS is a seed: it admits its listed originators until it is retired; the owner's grants are the rows, listed by bsv-wallet origins`. Unset seeds nothing, and every page is asked per power. `bsv-wallet origin-revoke` of a listed originator with no row names its `ALLOWED_ORIGINS` line. The host routes (`/permission-audit`, `/permission-audit/reset`, `/chain-feed`, `/tx/{txid}`) answer the owner (no `Origin`, a loopback `Host`, the bearer under `AUTH_TOKEN`) or the seed, never a page's grant. The listed callers: `bsv-wallet-mcp`'s `chain_feed_status` and `tx_word` now send no `Origin` to the host routes (the owner's admission, with `WALLET_AUTH_TOKEN` as the bearer when set); the SwiftBar health checks call `isAuthenticated`, an open door, and need no line; the stack's witness job's `GET /tx/{txid}` with `Origin: http://localhost` needs its `localhost` line, or calls with no `Origin` from loopback as the owner; the e2e harness's wallets keep `e2e-test` listed, its `audited` reads `/permission-audit` as the owner with no `Origin`, and its browser gate's wallet D runs with the variable unset.

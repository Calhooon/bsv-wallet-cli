- **A prepared payment's send is refused by name, not answered with a payment nobody asked for (#147).** A payer on the reference's client (`@bsv/sdk` from 4.1.0, `AuthFetch`) pays in two `createAction` calls: the payment prepared with `noSend: true`, then a submitting call naming it in `options.sendWith` with no inputs or outputs. Before, this host dropped `sendWith`, so the submitting call arrived as an empty `createAction`: a new transaction was built from the wallet's coins and posted, a fee was spent, the prepared payment was never sent, and the payer's client reported an unknown outcome and never sent the paid request. Now a `createAction` naming a non-empty `sendWith` or `noSendChange` is refused with 501 `SEND_WITH_NOT_SUPPORTED`, before anything is built, stored or posted; its message says that this host cannot yet send a prepared payment, that the prepared transaction was not sent and is still reserved under its txid, and that `abortAction` with its txid as the reference frees its coins. The host never aborts it for the payer: a `noSend` transaction may already be in a counterparty's hands. An absent field, `null` or `[]` is answered as before. The pass-through of `sendWith` follows when the wallet below this host answers the call. Wire rows 292 and 293 record the refusal; the MCP's `create_action` tool forwards `options` as before and its text says what the host answers.

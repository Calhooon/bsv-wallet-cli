- **The open doors are bounded per site (#541)**: a page the seed (`ALLOWED_ORIGINS`) does not list is still answered at the open doors and under a level-0 protocol with no grant (W15 (i)), now within a bound per originator (the hostname, every port one site), kept in the process's memory and never in the store: `isAuthenticated` and `waitForAuthentication` answer a site once a minute each (the served wallet keeps no session of its own); `getHeight`, `getHeaderForHeight`, `getNetwork` and `getVersion` together answer 60 a minute with a burst of 10; a protocol power at security level 0 answers 60 a minute with a burst of 10 on a bound of its own. Past a bound the call is answered at once `429 {"code": "ERR_RATE_LIMITED", "message": ...}`, the message naming the door and the window, with `Retry-After` (whole seconds, the wait rounded up) and the CORS echo, `retry-after` exposed to the page; no row is written. A seed-listed originator, the owner's routes and the host routes' owner (no `Origin`) are outside the bound; a restart resets it.
- **The library's `ServerConfig` gains a public field, `bounds: server::open_bounds::OpenBounds` (#541)**: the open doors' counters, shared by every clone; `ServerConfig::default` and `ServerConfig::from_config` set a fresh one. `OpenBounds::stopped()` is a bound whose clock stands still (the wire harness's worlds). A struct literal of `ServerConfig` outside the crate must now name the field (or take `..Default::default()`).

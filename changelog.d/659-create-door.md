- **An action the wallet signs has a tracker word from its signing (#659, the create door's word).** Every action this wallet signs is `built` from the moment it is signed, and `announced` once a broadcaster took it (read from the broadcast memory's row for the transaction, which names the broadcaster): the served `createAction` and `signAction`, and `send`, `drain`, `split`, `gift-send` and the respend's replacement. A `noSend` action and one that accepts delayed broadcast are `built` until sent; an unsigned template has no word until `signAction` signs it. Before, such an action had no tracker row until the tracker's next pass, so `GET /tx/{txid}` answered 404 and the spend gate read its change's creator as `unknown`; a `noSend` action never had one. What a caller meets: `GET /tx/{txid}` answers 200 with `word` `built` or `announced` right after the action is signed. No offer moves: the gate offers the wallet's own unmined change under `SPEND_OWN_CHANGE` as it did, and the gate's vectors replay at their pin with 0 divergences. A failure to write the row never fails the action; it is logged as `signed_word_not_written`. A store that already holds actions needs no step: the pass adopts each unmined one at `built`, as before.

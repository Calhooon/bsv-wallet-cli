- **`split` and `gift-claim` refuse by name before the post a spend the wallet could not record (#634).** Both post and then record through `internalizeAction`, which refuses a payment spending a coin mined before Genesis as not judged; on this line, as on the stable line through 0.18.8, `split` posted and then printed `Warning: the split outputs were not self-internalized`, and `gift-claim` posted and answered `"recorded": false`. Now `split` does not spend a coin whose transaction the wallet's own records place below the Genesis height its door judges by, nor the unmined change of a spend of one: such a coin is held for `split` alone, its reason `mined before Genesis: coin height <h> is before Genesis height <g>; split does not offer it` (or `... <txid> is not mined yet and spends a coin of height <h> ...`). When the other coins cover the split it spends them and prints `Not spent: <txid>.<vout> <sats> sats: <reason>` (`notSpent` under `--json`); when they do not, it refuses with the gate's own refusal, `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` (`INPUT_NOT_OFFERED` when `--from` names the coin), the coin under `held`, the message ending in why and that a plain `send` of the coin is not held for this reason; nothing is signed or posted. `gift-claim` refuses before the post: `gift-claim refused, nothing posted: the claim spends the gift's deposit <txid>, which its proof places at height <h>, before Genesis height <g>, and internalizeAction refuses a payment spending a coin mined before Genesis as not judged, so the wallet could not record the claim.`; nothing posted, no tracker row. Every other command's offer is unchanged.

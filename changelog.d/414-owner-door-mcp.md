- **The owner's door over the per-origin grants (#414, the design's L3):** four host routes on the served wallet, written through `store::origin_grants` alone: `GET /origins` answers `{"grants": [...], "pending": [...]}` (every grant row, the open pending requests and the last 50 answered, in `bsv-wallet origins --json`'s keys), `POST /origins/answer` (`{"pendingId", "answer": "grant"|"once"|"deny", "expiry"?, "amount"?}`; of two answers to one request the second is 409 `CONFLICT` naming the first), `POST /origins/grant` (`{"originator", "scope", "expiry"?, "amount"?}`) and `POST /origins/revoke` (`{"originator", "scope"?}`, no scope revoking both; 409 naming an originator with no live row). They are the owner's, never a page's: outside the browser gate and the CORS layer, admitted only with no `Origin` header and a loopback `Host`, then the bearer under `AUTH_TOKEN`; a call with an `Origin`, its preflight, or a `Host` beyond loopback is 403 `ACCESS_DENIED` naming the rule. `bsv-wallet-mcp` gains three tools over them, `list_origins`, `answer_origin` and `revoke_origin` (37 tools), sent with no `Origin` header and with the new `WALLET_AUTH_TOKEN` (printed `<set>` by `--config`) as the bearer when set. The doors still admit by `ALLOWED_ORIGINS`.

- **The open doors' bound counts only what it answers (#556)**: a wrong-method call at an open door (a `GET` at the POST door `getHeaderForHeight`, a `PUT` at a status door) is the router's `405`, neither counted in the probe record nor charged to the site's bound.
- **A level-0 call is charged once (#556)**: a call whose door asks several protocol powers at security level 0 moves the level-0 bound once, and only when every power it asks has admitted it; a later refusal (a bound, an admin-reserved name, a revoked or denied power) leaves the bound and the probe record where they stood.

- **A `--testnet` wallet is one chain: `getNetwork` answers `testnet`, the chain its door judges by (#639).** Every wallet is built on the chain its context names, the same word `init` writes in the store's `chain` setting from `--testnet`, which the toolbox's `internalizeAction` judges a received payment by. What moves for a caller on testnet: the served `GET /getNetwork` and `POST /getNetwork` (and the MCP's `get_network`) answer `{"network":"testnet"}` where they answered `{"network":"mainnet"}`; the door judges, as it did, under testnet's heights (a coin proven below Genesis 1,344,302 is not judged), and `split`'s hold and `gift-claim`'s check ask the same height, as they did. A store whose `chain` setting names the other chain is now refused at open, before anything is served or built, by every command that opens a wallet (`serve`, `daemon`, `serve-fleet`, `tick` and the commands with a context): `the wallet database <db> was created for chain test (its settings' chain) and this command runs on chain main: run it with --testnet`; a store whose word is `main` opened with `--testnet` is told the repair instead (#682). Before, such a store was opened: it answered mainnet and judged by its own setting's chain whatever the flag said. A testnet store whose setting still reads `main` (written by a `--testnet` `init` before the store took its chain from the flag) is refused the same way; `repair-chain` sets its word (#682). Nothing moves on mainnet: a wallet without `--testnet` answers `mainnet` and judges under mainnet's heights as before, and every recorded wire answer replays unchanged.

- **A received payment's transaction has a tracker word from the moment it is taken in (#645, the door's word and the take).** After `internalizeAction` (the served door, and `fund`, `receive`, `sync`, `split`, `send` to the wallet's own address and `gift-claim`), a payment that arrived with no proof is `built`, and `announced` once a broadcaster took the wallet's own post of it or a `receive` courier handed it over unmined. Before, `GET /tx/{txid}` answered 404 for it until the tracker's next pass, and a pass left it `built` when the broadcast memory held no row for the take. What a caller meets: `GET /tx/{txid}` answers 200 with `word` `built` or `announced` right after the door; a held received coin in `createAction`'s 402 carries `word` `announced` where it carried `unknown`. No offer moves: what `allowPending: received` offers is as it was, and the gate's vectors replay at their pin with 0 divergences. A payment that arrives with its proof reads `mined` as before. A store that already holds received payments needs no step: the pass gives each its row and now hears its take from the wallet's own records. One word per transaction: two users of one store who receive the same transaction read the same word.

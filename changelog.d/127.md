- **`docs/ratchets.json` names its instrument; `tools/ratchet --check` refuses a record another tool made (#127).**
  The CI job measured with the PR's own `tools/ratchet` and
  `tools/ratchet.d/clippy.toml` against a record the base's tool made, so a
  PR that edited the tool changed what is counted and only the diff showed
  it. The record (schema 2) now carries `tool`: the sha256 of both files as
  HEAD holds them. `--check` exits 1 when the fingerprint differs from the
  tree's tool's, asking for a re-record in the commit that changes the tool,
  with the reason in its message. A `--base` record of another tool or an
  older schema is not a refusal: a `::warning::` says the base comparison is
  skipped, and the tree is still checked against its own record. A schema
  move is explicit: `--record --schema-move`. The record was re-recorded once
  under schema 2 at the pinned toolchain; four numbers fell to beta's tree
  (clippy_pedantic 834 to 825, functions_over_60_lines 89 to 86,
  duplicated_tokens 32643 to 32572, missing_docs 150 to 148).

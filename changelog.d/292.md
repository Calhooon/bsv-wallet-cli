- **A proven creator whose stored proof fails its check no longer holds back every other adoption (#292).** The tick's adoption of the toolbox's proven creators left a creator whose `proven_txs` path does not parse, does not hold for its txid, or does not meet the header unstored and reported, so every pass selected it again; 100 such rows (the per-pass bound) held back every new creator and every dead-row revival for good. Each failure is now stored once and not selected again until its check is due: a new creator whose path does not parse or does not hold is tracked without it (its row holds the word its hints give, `unknown` received or `built` made, and no path; a mismatched root keeps its `proofFailed` row, as before); a `rejected`, `conflicted` or `abandoned` row keeps its word, is never revived by a failed check, and stores no path and the tracker's re-ask (`proof_failed@<height>` when the path parsed). Both carry one ask with the tick's doubling pause. Such a row is checked again once that pause passes (so a path the toolbox rewrites, such as its JSON-to-BUMP repair, is read, and a new creator whose path then holds is adopted as `mined`) or on a pass whose header moved at or below the proof's height, and the fault is reported once per check. A header the service could not give, or a height above its tip, still waits for the next pass. `/tx/{txid}` for a new creator tracked without its path serves its row's word (`unknown` or `built`) where it was 404 before, since the row is what keeps it out of the bound. The gate's reading of a made creator tracked without its path moves its word from `unknown` to `built`, shown in a refusal's `held[].word`; its door, its fault and its hold reason do not move, and a dead row's reading does not move. No fixture or vector moves. New `tracker_host::dead_proof_tests` cover the starvation (100 unparseable creators ahead of a new creator and a revivable dead row: both adopted on the second pass, which reports no fault), a moved header reviving a marked row, a marked row checked again after its pause and revived from a rewritten path, a new creator adopted after the toolbox repairs its JSON path, and a made creator's reading; two of #215's dead-row tests now expect the mark.

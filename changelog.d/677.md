- **Coins held by a prepared, unsent payment are shown, and one command frees them (#673).** A `noSend` payment (`createAction` with `noSend: true`, as a paying client prepares one) is signed and never sent. Until it is sent or aborted it keeps the coins its inputs spend reserved, and nothing ages it out; a payer whose client submits it by `sendWith` is refused by this host (#147) and is left with one. Before, nothing at the command line showed it: `bsv-wallet actions` printed it as one `nosend` line among the others, and `balance` read lower by the payment and its fee with no word of why. Now `bsv-wallet actions` marks each prepared payment under its line with the sats its inputs hold, its age, its whole txid and its reference, and a last line totals the hold; under `--json` a prepared action's object carries an added `prepared` key and the listing an added `preparedTotal`, every other key as before. `bsv-wallet actions --prepared` lists only the prepared payments. `bsv-wallet abort-action <txid>` (or its reference) aborts the one payment it names through the wallet's `abortAction`: its coins are spendable again and the wallet never sends it. A payment a broadcaster took is never aborted (`CONFLICT`). An abort frees this wallet's coins and nothing more: a prepared transaction whose bytes were handed to someone else can still be posted by them, so abort only a payment that was not handed over. The 501 `SEND_WITH_NOT_SUPPORTED` message names both commands and now says "if any" of the named prepared payment; the MCP's `abort_action` says the same.

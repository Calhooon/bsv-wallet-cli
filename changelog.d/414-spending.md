- **The spending power: a page's `createAction` is held for the owner's answer to its spend, once or under a monthly ceiling (#414, the design's L6):** a call whose outputs sum to more than 0 satoshis asks the spending power for that sum (the requested outputs' satoshis before the wallet builds; the network fee is not counted, and a call whose outputs sum to 0 asks none). A page with no spending row is held on a pending request of kind `spending` (`spend 2500 satoshis`, the page's description beside it), shown by `bsv-wallet origins`, the owner's routes, `bsv-wallet-mcp`'s `list_origins` and the inspector. The owner answers it **once** (`bsv-wallet origin-grant #ID --once`, `answer: "once"`): the one call goes on and no grant is written, so the next spend is asked again; **grant with a ceiling** (`bsv-wallet origin-grant #ID --amount SATS`, `answer: "grant"` with `amount`, or `bsv-wallet origin-grant <originator> --kind spending --amount SATS` directly): a spending row whose ceiling is the satoshis the site may spend each calendar month in UTC, never expiring; or **deny**: 400 `Permission denied.`. A site holding a spending row spends without a prompt while this month's spends plus the call's amount are at most the ceiling (equal is admitted); a call that would pass it is held as a renewal (`spend 2500 satoshis past this month's ceiling of 60000 (58000 spent)`), and a grant answering it sets the new ceiling. Two calls of one site can never both pass a ceiling one of them fills: the check and the month's tally are made under the wallet's spending lock, which a held call lets go while it waits. The tally counts each admitted spend once the wallet built it: a template's spend is counted from `createAction` and keeps its count when `signAction` signs it; an `abortAction` that succeeds takes the spend out of the month; a transaction a broadcaster took is never aborted and stays counted. A seed-listed originator (`ALLOWED_ORIGINS`) spends as before, with no ceiling.

- **The per-origin grants from the command line (#414, the design's L2):** four commands over the store's grant rows, written through `store::origin_grants` alone: `bsv-wallet origins [--all]` lists every grant row (`granted`, `expired` or `revoked`; a spending row with its monthly ceiling and the month's tally) and the open pending requests (`--all` adds the answered ones), with `--json` printing `{"grants": [...], "pending": [...]}` in the design's keys, times in unix seconds; `bsv-wallet origin-grant <originator | #pending-id> [--scope wallet|spending] [--amount SATS] [--expires SECONDS|never] [--once]` grants an originator directly or answers a pending request (`--amount` required for spending unless `--once`; an originator with a scheme, a path or credentials is refused naming the rule it breaks); `bsv-wallet origin-deny <#pending-id>` denies one; `bsv-wallet origin-revoke <originator> [--scope wallet|spending]` stamps the live rows and exits 1 naming the originator when it holds none. The library gains `store::origin_grants::listing` and `Listing`. No door changes: the served doors still admit by `ALLOWED_ORIGINS`.

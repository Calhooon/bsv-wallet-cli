- **The probe record (#539):** every answer the browser gate gives a page with no row (the open doors `isAuthenticated`, `waitForAuthentication`, `getHeight`, `getHeaderForHeight`, `getNetwork`, `getVersion`, and any protocol at security level 0) is now counted per origin in a record of its own, never a grant: one row per originator, door and level-0 protocol with its count and its first and last times, at most 32 rows per originator (past it the rows seen longest ago fold into one remainder row, door `*`), written off the call's path so no call waits on it. `bsv-wallet origins` prints a `probe` line per row (`probe  new.example  getVersion  2 times  first <time>  last <time>`) after the grants and pending rows, and `--json`, `GET /origins`, the MCP's `list_origins` and the inspector's origins view carry a `probes` section (`originator`, `rawOriginator`, `door`, `power`, `count`, `firstSeen`, `lastSeen`, times in unix seconds). `origin-revoke` leaves the record. A store gains the `origin_probes` table on its next open.

- **The beta.9 pins: bsv-rs =0.9.0-beta.9, bsv-wallet-toolbox-rs =0.14.0-beta.9, bsv-tracker =0.9.0-beta.9 (#612).** The three move together onto bsv-rs's security prerelease. The toolbox's prerelease carries that pin alone. The tracker's carries it and the room made before its 0.9.0: `HostAction`, `Reask`, `HintStatus`, `Verdict` and `VerdictError` are `#[non_exhaustive]`, each `HintStatus` and `Reask` kind has a stable word (`label`, `LABELS`), and `HintStatus` has a serde form, tagged `status` by that word.
- **The tracker's words in place of the host's two hand maps (#612).** A stored tracker hint is the crate's own form of its status beside its source and time (`{source, status, <payload>, observed}`), in place of `StoredHint::of`'s and `StoredHint::hint`'s hand maps. The rows already stored read as they are and are written back identical; no row migrates. The re-ask a row stores and `/tx/:txid` serves takes each kind's word from `Reask::label`, and a kind this host does not name is shown by the crate's word alone. No served string moves.
- **An unreadable stored hint is surfaced, never silence (#612).** Until now a tracker row whose hints column did not parse was read with no hints, replayed without them and written back without them. Now such a row is held as stored: never replayed, never written back, and named among the tracker pass's faults with its txid and the column's parse error on every pass that selects it. `/tx/:txid` keeps serving the word and re-ask the row last stored (with no `tier`), the spend door offers nothing on it and names the hold, and a broadcaster's word heard for it is logged at warn (`tracker_row_held`) and not added.
- **Two answers move on purpose (#612).** A stored `mined` hint with no height and a stored `mempool_present` hint with no courier are refused (the row is held, as above), where they were read as `unknown`. A stored `rejected` hint with no reason, which was read as an empty reason, is refused as well. This host's `StoredHint::of` never wrote any of the three.
- **Security: GHSA-33fm-jcm5-4m3m (High), fixed in bsv-rs 0.9.0-beta.9.** The advisory names every bsv-rs prerelease through 0.9.0-beta.8. On this line, no door where another party's BEEF arrives reaches bsv-rs's linker: the toolbox's certificate and overlay lookups and `internalizeAction` read a BEEF's subject or entries without linking it, and `createAction`'s input BEEF, the couriers of `receive` and the gift commands parse and merge without linking. The CLI makes one linking read, in `send` to the wallet's own address (`find_atomic_transaction` with bsv-rs's default limits over the BEEF the wallet's own `createAction` returned). At this pin that read is also held to the new heap bound, so a payment's ancestry that would hold more than 56 MiB of heap while linking is refused there. `send` then answers by naming the transaction (it is already on the network) and the `fund` command that keeps it later.

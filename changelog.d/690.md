- **A received payment a broadcaster took is spendable at once; the hold is a host setting (#659, `api-break`).** The served `createAction` and `send`, `drain`, `split` and `gift-send` now offer a received payment that is not mined yet as soon as a broadcaster took it (its tracker word reads `announced` or `seen`), with no `allowPending`. Before, such a spend was refused 402 `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` with the coin held `received, no proof; offered under allowPending: received`. What moves for a caller:
  - `allowPending: received` (`--allow-pending received`) is still read and is no longer needed for such a coin. The scoped form, `allowPending: {"received": ["txid:vout"]}` (`--coin`), still narrows the offer to the received coins it names.
  - A received payment no broadcaster took (its word reads `built` or `unknown`) is held until a broadcaster takes it or it is mined, whatever the action says: `allowPending: received` no longer offers it. A refused `createAction` lists it under `held` with the reason `built; received, not taken by a broadcaster: offered once a broadcaster takes it or once mined`, and `send` prints the same line.
  - Our own change is offered from `built` on, as before under `SPEND_OWN_CHANGE=1`. Change whose action has no tracker word (`unknown`) is no longer offered; a refusal lists it `unknown; own change not signed: never offered`, and naming it as an input is 403 `INPUT_NOT_OFFERED`. Every action this wallet signs is `built` from its signing, so this is an unsigned template's change, or an action from before that the next tracker pass adopts.
  - A new host setting, `SPEND_RECEIVED`: `taken` (the default) or `hold`; any other value is refused at start naming the variable, and `bsv-wallet config` prints `taken (default)` or `hold`. `SPEND_RECEIVED=hold` keeps the word's rule: a received unmined payment is offered only to an action that says `allowPending: received`. It is stricter than before in one place: even with the word, a payment no broadcaster took is held until it is mined.

  **Received payments already in a wallet when it upgrades.** A payment taken in before this release carries no word until the wallet gives it one. The upgraded wallet does this once, at the next tracker pass of `daemon` or `serve` (or `bsv-wallet tracker-tick`): each unmined received payment gets the word `built`, and it moves to `announced` when the wallet's record of a broadcaster's answer shows that a broadcaster took it. A payment whose take left no such record keeps `built`, and so does one the gate reads before that pass (it reads `unknown`): it is held until it is mined, with the reason above in the `held` list of a refused `createAction` and in `send`'s message. Nothing is posted again on the upgrade's account, and no word but a broadcaster's recorded answer or the coin's checked proof frees it.

  The gate is replayed against the stack's wallet-gate vectors at their new pin (1,314 rows, 1,252 expressed, 0 divergences) under both settings. The wire corpus gains four rows (295 to 298); its `received-rule` world is now a host that sets `SPEND_RECEIVED=hold`, so no recorded row moves.

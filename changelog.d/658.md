- **A coin the store marks not spendable: four answers pinned by tests, none changed (#646).** A caller of `POST /createAction` meets the answers this line already gave. Over a wallet whose only coin is mined and marked not spendable with no spender (the record `reconcile-outputs --execute`, `cleanup-abandoned --execute` and the spend guard write), a signed action with `options.acceptDelayedBroadcast: true` answers 402 `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`, `offeredSatoshis` 0, and `held[]` carries the coin with the word `lost` and the reason `lost: the store marks it not spendable`; nothing is allocated and no transaction is stored for the daemon's monitor to post later (wire row 291, in a world of its own, `not-spendable`). The same action posted at once is refused with the coin named the same way. A received coin the spend guard holds is refused under `allowPending: received`, posted at once and accepting delayed broadcast alike, and the guard's hold is left as it wrote it. These are the answers of the hold of #165; the stable line's 0.18.9 has no such hold (#646), and the tests are written here so that it can take them with the hold. The tests are `src/spend_gate/tests/not_spendable.rs`; docs/WIRE.md has the section.
